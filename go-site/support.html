EmbraceHealthAI Companion support

The Companion is the phone app for EmbraceHealthAI and iStride. It tracks runs, rides and walks with your screen locked and saves them to your iStride account.

Contact us

Email support@embracehealth.ai or call (312) 847-1858.

Signing in

Use your EmbraceHealthAI email and password. Opening the Companion from EmbraceHealthAI or iStride on your phone signs you in automatically. Forgot your password? Reset it at app.embracehealth.ai.

Tracking with your screen locked

  • Allow location While Using the App. The Companion only uses location during an activity you start, and a blue bar or notice shows while it does.
  • Keep Low Power Mode off during activities for the steadiest GPS.
  • No signal? Your activity is saved on the phone and uploads when you're back online.

Deleting your account

  1. In the Companion: Settings (the gear) → Delete my account. Or on the web: My Account → Delete My Account at app.embracehealth.ai.
  2. Type DELETE and confirm. You're signed out everywhere at once.
  3. After 30 days we erase your personal data. Email us before then to change your mind. Records the law requires us to keep, such as purchases and lab orders, are kept without your name, email or login.

Can't sign in? Email support@embracehealth.ai from your account's email address and we'll delete it for you.
